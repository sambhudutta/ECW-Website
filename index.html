The initiative

Power progress responsibly

VIT-AP University proudly announces Energy Conservation Week, an initiative designed to inspire responsible energy use and innovative approaches to cost optimisation.

Guided by the vision of Swarna Andhra Vision 2047 – Cost Optimisation: Energy and Fuel, the week brings together students, faculty and the wider community to promote efficiency, sustainability and lasting positive change.
